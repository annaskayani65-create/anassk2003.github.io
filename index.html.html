Advancing Plant Protection Through Molecular Diagnostics & Field Epidemiology

I am Anas Sajid, an Agriculture graduate specializing in Plant Pathology from Pir Mehr Ali Shah (PMAS) Arid Agriculture University Rawalpindi (CGPA 3.39/4.00, with strong senior-year specialization GPAs of 3.94 in Semester 7 and 4.00 in Semester 8).

Through my research internship at the National Agricultural Research Centre (NARC), Islamabad under the Pulses Program (CSI), I acquired hands-on laboratory proficiency in DNA/RNA extraction, PCR, RT-qPCR workflows, ELISA, Agarose Gel Electrophoresis, SDS-PAGE, and pure-culture fungal isolation and purification of Fusarium spp. from chickpea.

I am actively seeking a Master's degree scholarship and research supervision to contribute to cutting-edge plant pathology and molecular host-pathogen research.
